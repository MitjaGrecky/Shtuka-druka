## Checked (cloud task #27, rev 0.2, datasheets)
- **ADC128S102:** SPI mode 0/3, 16 SCLK per conversion, SCLK 8–16 MHz (RP2350 SPI0 at 16 MHz = 1 Msps), VA = VD = reference through the ferrite. VA bypass is 1 µF + 0.1 µF at the pins (datasheet), so one 100 nF became 1 µF. The pad dividers hang on +3V3A before the ferrite, the ADC on VAP after it: ferrite DCR × ADC current is far below 1 LSB, so the measurement stays ratiometric.
- **Anti-alias 1 k + 1 nF:** the ADC input (33 pF) takes charge from the 1 nF, so the first read of a channel after another one is off by up to 33/1033 = 3.2 % of the step between them. Firmware reads each channel twice per column and keeps the second (costs 8 µs per column).
- **Settling:** after a column change the 1 nF recharges through 1 k + 74HC4051 Ron + the pad node (10 k ‖ FSR, up to 5 k): τ ≈ 6–7 µs, 12 bit needs 8.3 τ ≈ 55 µs. A full 8 × 8 scan is about 0.5 ms (≈ 2 kHz), not 130 µs; the 8 rows are read in parallel by the 8 ADC channels.
- **74HC4051 at 3.3 V:** the datasheet gives Ron only for 2.0 V (150 Ω typ), 4.5 V (90 Ω typ, 160 Ω max) and 6 V; at 3.3 V expect 120–150 Ω. The ADC draws no DC current, so Ron adds no gain error; it only adds to τ. Leakage ±0.1 µA per channel at 25 °C, ±1 µA worst over temperature (the datasheet figures are for 10 V) × 10 k ≤ 10 mV ≈ 12 LSB worst case, typically a few LSB. Supply 2–10 V, HC (not HCT) inputs are fine from 3.3 V GPIO.
- **LCSC:** C2943042 from rev 0.1 could not be confirmed (LCSC pages are not reachable from the cloud). Replaced by C5645, Nexperia 74HC4051PW,118 (TSSOP-16, as the footprint). Check stock and Basic/Extended when ordering.
- **10 k pull-down:** node = 3.3 V × 10 k / (10 k + R). R = 1 k → 3.0 V; 10 k → 1.65 V; 100 k → 0.30 V (370 LSB); 1 M → 33 mV (41 LSB); open (> 10 M) → ≈ 3 mV (noise level). Light touch (≈ 100 kΩ for an FSR 402-class sensor) is visible; the resistor is a good fit. A larger R (47 k) would lift the light-touch end but flatten the heavy end: keep 10 k until the real Velostat is measured.
- **I²C addresses:** TCA9546A 0x73 (decks 0x70–0x72), LP5864 on chip address bits `1 0 0 ADDR1 ADDR0` (four chips A–D on channel 0, E on channel 2), Trill Bar 0x20 (default), INA226 0x40. No address is used twice anywhere, and the INA226 and Trill B share channel 3 only with each other. Pull-ups: 4.7 k on the main bus and on every used mux channel (the Trill Bar has none).
- **Trill Bar connector:** the sensor uses a Qwiic/SH 1.0 connector, order GND, 3.3 V, SDA, SCL. Our PH4 header (rev 0.1: SDA, SCL, 3.3 V, GND) now follows that order so a plain 1:1 SH–PH cable works.
- **RP2350A block:** pin map identical to R2 (compared in code), and R2 follows the Raspberry Pi minimal design (3.3 µH inductor, 33 Ω + 4.7 µF on VREG_AVDD, 4.7 µF DVDD, 12 MHz with 1 k, 15 pF, W25Q128 on QSPI, 10 k RUN).
- **Rev 0.5 (16.11.2026, R168):** JST PH headers are side-entry S3B/S4B-PH-K-S (4.8 mm high, C157929 / C157926), the power header is the single-row right-angle Micro-Fit (mated height 6.98 mm per the Molex listing: the drawing itself was not opened, check before ordering), bulk capacitors 100 µF 16 V 6.3 × 5.4 (C7469977). All four in stock at JLCPCB on 16.11.2026. The 1.27 mm bus headers J4–J7 (2×7, 2×8) are right-angle too: hanxia HX PZ1.27-2x7P WZ (C22438113, 2 735 in stock) and 2x8P WZ (C22438114, 1 854), 3.4 mm high (HX drawing: body 3.4 x 1.0, pins 4.0 out), 17-19 cents. Unshrouded, no key: pin 1 is marked on the silk, the ribbon plug goes on with the red edge on pin 1. They open towards the rear edge (towards M1; the decks' cables run on along the gaps).
- **+5 V through one Micro-Fit (three +5 V pins, as before):** worst case 5.6 A (PW README) over three +5 V pins = 1.9 A per pin, within what Micro-Fit 3.0 contacts are rated for (about 5 A per circuit with tin plating and 18 AWG, from memory: confirm on the Molex 43045 sheet). The weak places are the wire (use 18 AWG or thicker) and the copper: the +5V_PANEL pour on the PCB must carry 5.6 A (≥ 4 mm wide on 1 oz outer layers, or a plane). The 2 × 100 µF + 10 µF on the input stay.
