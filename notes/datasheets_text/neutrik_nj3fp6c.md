Locking 1/4" phone jack, D-size shell, nickel metal

housing and silver contacts

The 3 pole phone jack panel mount version provides

stable and secure connections where accidental

disconnect or shock and vibration may occur. It

latches with standard A-gauge mono or stereo

plugs. Plug locks-in automatically when mating and

unlocks only by pressing red release tab. Standard

D size front panel appearance.
