Reversible USB 2.0 gender changer (type A and B),

Due to the rising digitisation the interface problems

between audio/video networks, IT equipment, MP3

camcorders, etc. increase. A rugged solution is

needed.

Neutrik's reversible USB gender changer, mounted

in the standardized D-shape housing, and the

rugged patch cables solve this problem.
